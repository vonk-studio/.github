# Hey, we're VONK

**We turn ambitious ideas into working technology.**

We're an independent product design and development studio based in the Netherlands.

From mobile applications and embedded systems to custom hardware and interactive experiences, we bring together software, 
electronics, and creative engineering to build things that make a difference.

### What we do

- **Software Development** — Native applications, custom platforms, and connected experiences.
- **Hardware & Embedded Systems** — Electronics, sensors, device integration, and custom-built hardware.
- **Product Engineering** — From early prototypes and proof of concepts to production-ready solutions.
- **Interactive Technology** — Bringing physical and digital experiences together in unexpected ways.
- **Connected Products** — Real-time data, remote monitoring, and intelligent device management.

### Some of our work

We've had the opportunity to build technology for some remarkable projects:

- **Odido Startphone** — A custom Android experience helping children develop healthy smartphone habits.
- **Stedin eKlok** — A connected outdoor installation that makes electricity grid demand visible to the public.
- **Samsung SmartSuit** — Sensor-integrated sportswear providing real-time performance insights for Olympic short-track athletes.
- **Samsung FastFrame** — A connected BMX training system combining embedded sensors with performance analytics.
- **Philips AED Shirt** — Wearable LED technology helping raise awareness of life-saving AED accessibility.

Explore more of our work at **[vonk.studio](https://vonk.studio)**.

### How we work

We love solving problems that don't come with an off-the-shelf solution.

Whether it's writing software, integrating sensors, designing electronics, or building something entirely new, 
we focus on making technology work in the real world.

**Curiosity drives us. Engineering makes it happen.**
